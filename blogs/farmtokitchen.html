From Farm to Kitchen: How Our Food Is Produced

A look at the usual journey of a grain product—and the details that help make that journey clearer.

30 September 2026By Vraj Naturals EditorialFarming & Farmers
Home/Journal/Farm to Kitchen

Every grain product passes through several hands and steps before it reaches a kitchen. The exact chain depends on the crop, region, producer and product. The outline below describes common stages; it is not a claim about the sourcing of any particular Vraj Naturals product.

Harvest and handling

When a crop is ready, it is harvested and moved for drying or other preparation as needed. Keeping grain clean and dry during handling matters for quality. Storage conditions and the time between harvest and processing can vary widely.

Cleaning and processing

Before milling or packing, grain is commonly cleaned to remove unwanted material. Some crops may be de-hulled or otherwise processed. Flour production adds a milling step, while rice and pulses can involve their own preparation and grading stages.

Milling, packing and labels

How a grain is milled affects the texture of the resulting flour. Finished products are packed in different sizes for retail or bulk use. Check the label for the product name, ingredients, net quantity, dates and the contact details required for the product.

Questions worth asking

  • What grain or variety is in this pack?
  • Where was it grown or processed, if that information is available?
  • Is it whole grain, milled flour, polished rice or another form?
  • How should I store and prepare it?

Clear answers help customers make informed choices and help responsible producers explain their work. If a detail is not listed, ask the seller rather than assuming that all products with a similar name share the same source or process.
