From Seed to Home: The Sona Moti Journey

A closer look at the work and care behind our Sona Moti wheat, from preparing the seed to packing the flour.

9 November 2026By Vraj Naturals EditorialFarming & Farmers
Home/Journal/Sona Moti journey

Good food has a story that starts long before it reaches the kitchen. This is the journey we follow with Sona Moti wheat at Vraj Naturals. The short films below show six stages, from seed preparation and sowing through to harvest and packing.

01. Seed treatment

Every harvest begins with carefully prepared seeds and living soil. Seed preparation is the first step in getting ready for sowing.

02. Sowing

The prepared seed is sown on our farms as the growing season begins.

03. Growth

A crop takes time. Soil, water, weather, and attentive care shape its progress through the field.

04. Jeevaamrit

Jeevaamrit-based farming practices are part of our approach to nourishing and caring for the soil.

05. Harvesting

The wheat is harvested when the crop reaches maturity. This is when the work in the field becomes grain ready for the next stages.

06. Milling and packaging

The grain is milled into atta and packed carefully before it makes its way to your home. We prepare and pack in small batches.

From our fields to your kitchen

We believe customers should be able to learn more about where their food comes from and how it is handled. This journal is one way to share that journey and the people and practices behind it.
