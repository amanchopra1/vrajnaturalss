THE DETAILS

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Who we are

Vraj Naturals operates this website and handles the information described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, email info@vrajnaturals.com or write to DSS 105, 3, Near Sehgal Hospital, HUDA Sector, Fatehabad, Haryana 125050, India.

2. Information we collect

When you place an order or request a subscription, we collect the name, email address, phone number and delivery address you provide, along with the selected products, quantities, prices, delivery charges, schedule, order reference and payment status. Razorpay processes payment authorisation and payment details through its checkout. The website does not ask you to send card or bank credentials to Vraj Naturals.

If you contact us, we use the information in your message to respond. If you join The Vraj Circle, the contact details and marketing preference you submit are recorded in a Google spreadsheet.

3. How we use information

We use order information to create and manage checkouts, confirm payment status, arrange delivery, provide support, handle refunds or order issues, prevent misuse and keep business records. If you begin a checkout and provide your email, we may send one reminder about that unfinished checkout after 24 minutes. This is a service message about the checkout, not consent to promotional marketing.

We use The Vraj Circle marketing preference only as recorded. If you have not opted in, we do not treat your signup as permission for promotional email or WhatsApp messages.

4. Service providers and sharing

We share only the information needed with the providers that support the service: Razorpay for payment processing, Cloudflare for website and checkout infrastructure, and Google services for order records and email delivery. We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Retention and security

We retain order, support and signup records for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, resolve questions and meet record-keeping obligations. Access to these systems is limited to people who need it to operate Vraj Naturals. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided. We will review the request and may need to retain records where required for an order, dispute or legal obligation. To update or withdraw a Vraj Circle marketing preference, email info@vrajnaturals.com.

7. Children

The website is intended for adults placing orders for delivery. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information without appropriate permission.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The latest version and update date will appear on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email info@vrajnaturals.com or write to DSS 105, 3, Near Sehgal Hospital, HUDA Sector, Fatehabad, Haryana 125050, India.
